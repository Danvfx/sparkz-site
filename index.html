About Us

Team name

Sparkz, a student business of Grade 12 ABM at La Salle College Antipolo.

The idea

Sparkz is a build-your-own mocktail. Every order is a chilled Sprite-based drink in a sealed cup, plus a 50ml flavor you pour in yourself. Four bases and six tops make 24 combinations. It's ₱80 for any of them.

Our mission

Sparkz is dedicated to giving the LSCA community back the break time that walking and lining up take from them, by delivering a fresh, customisable mocktail to where they already are.

Our vision

Sparkz aims to be LSCA's go-to drink brand for flavours that feel local and access that beats the canteen, and to build a repeatable model that future batches of students can adopt and improve.

Why we chose this product

We kept seeing the same thing at break. People wanted something cold, but the canteen was a long walk uphill, and the line ate whatever time was left. So they skipped it. We figured the drink should come to them.

How it connects to SDG 12

Sparkz makes drinks to confirmed pre-orders, pre-portions every flavor, uses sugarcane straws, and takes empty cups back. That's Responsible Consumption and Production. Our pitch also connects Sparkz to SDG 8 and SDG 9.

    SDG 12: Responsible Consumption and Production

    SDG 12 is about making and buying things in ways that waste less.

    The issue

    Single-use plastic. In the Philippines it shows up in sachets, shopping bags, cups and straws. On our campus, the everyday version is a drink cup and a straw that get used for one break and then thrown away.

    Why it matters

    The numbers below come from published sources. Each one links to its reference.

    2.7 million tons

    of plastic waste a year in the Philippines, and an estimated 20% ends up in the ocean.

    36%

    A 2021 model estimated that the Philippines accounts for about 36% of the plastic rivers carry into the ocean worldwide. Seven of the ten highest-emitting rivers in that model are in the Philippines. It's a model estimate, and the authors note real uncertainty.

    163 million

    plastic sachets used every day, plus 48 million shopping bags and 45 million thin plastic bags. These are 2019 figures.

    28%

    of key plastic resins were recycled in the Philippines in 2019.

    20% to 80%

    The Extended Producer Responsibility Act of 2022 requires large companies to recover a rising share of their plastic packaging: 20% by the end of 2023, up to 80% by the end of 2028.

    Target 12.5

    "Substantially reduce waste generation through prevention, reduction, recycling and reuse" by 2030.

        Questions

        What is Sparkz?

        A build-your-own mocktail: a chilled Sprite-based drink in a sealed 500ml cup, plus a 50ml flavor you pour in yourself.

        Is there alcohol in it?

        No. It's a mocktail.

        How much is it?

        ₱80 for any drink, signatures included.

        How many combinations are there?

        4 bases times 6 tops, so 24.

        How do I order?

        Pre-order on the form, buy at the stall, catch a roaming seller, or pre-order with delivery.

        When do pre-orders close?

        One hour before break. They open the day before.

        How do I pay?

        Pre-orders: GCash when you order. Stall: cash or GCash.

        Can you bring it to my classroom?

        Yes. Pre-order, choose delivery, and pin your classroom, building, or common area on the form.

        Where is the stall?

        Br. Donato Hall, La Salle College Antipolo.

        What is the straw made of?

        Sugarcane bagasse, not plastic.

        What do I do with the empty cup?

        Bring it back to the stall. We collect empty cups.

        Allergens and ingredients?

        We're confirming the full ingredient list. If you have an allergy, ask us at the stall before you order.
